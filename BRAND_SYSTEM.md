# Chef KnifeWorks Brand System

This file is the canonical visual and messaging reference for Chef KnifeWorks web and customer-facing digital experiences.

## Brand idea
Chef KnifeWorks should feel like a skilled craftsman's sharpening studio with the standards of a professional kitchen: warm, precise, confident, useful, and personal.

Avoid generic SaaS styling, faux-luxury steakhouse styling, novelty knife graphics, excessive gradients, and competing calls to action.

## Core palette
- Honed Sage — #8B9A88 — primary accent and primary CTA
- Steel Gray — #4A5557 — secondary surfaces, borders and supporting accents
- Whetstone Cream — #F5F3ED — light background and warm text
- Damascus Bronze — #C8754E — sparing secondary accent only
- Carbon Black — #2C2C2C — primary dark background and text
- Edge White — #FFFFFF — high-contrast text and utility use

### Color hierarchy
1. Carbon Black + Whetstone Cream establish the base.
2. Honed Sage is the dominant action/accent color.
3. Steel Gray supports structure.
4. Damascus Bronze is used sparingly for warmth, not as the main brand color.

## Typography
- Display/headlines: Della Respira
- Body, navigation, buttons and UI: Montserrat

Headlines should feel editorial and crafted, while body copy should remain extremely readable on mobile.

## Brand language
Primary brand name: Chef KnifeWorks
Primary tagline: Feel the Difference Sharp Can Make.
Supporting line: Precision · Craft · Performance

Customer journey vocabulary:
- Book Your Arrival
- Reserve
- Arrive
- Sharpen
- Depart

Use the arrival/departure metaphor only when it makes the process clearer. Never let the concept make booking or arrival instructions harder to understand.

## Positioning
Chef KnifeWorks is a professional home sharpening studio in Maple Grove, Minnesota. It is not a retail storefront. Present this confidently and intentionally; do not apologize for the home-studio model.

## Calls to action
Primary CTA: Book Your Arrival
Secondary CTAs: See How It Works, Call, Contact

The public homepage should not compete with booking by prominently presenting referrals, gift cards, tracking, check-in, CRM or staff tools.

## Logo rule
No canonical production logo file currently exists in this repository. Until the approved logo artwork is added, use the Chef KnifeWorks typographic wordmark with the small CKW monogram treatment consistently. Do not invent alternate logos.

When approved logo artwork is added, store one master SVG plus appropriate web exports in a dedicated public/brand directory and replace the temporary monogram in Header and Footer from that single source.

## Photography
Prefer real Chef KnifeWorks photography: actual knives, sharpening process, studio, finished edges, intake/pickup experience, and owner-at-work images. Avoid generic stock knife imagery wherever possible.

## Voice
Professional, hospitable, confident, practical, lightly playful. Craft first. Clarity before cleverness.
